Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information DuoVoice Studio (“the site”, “we”) handles when you use it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information you give us

  • Scripts. The text you enter is processed on our server and sent to a third-party text-to-speech provider so that audio can be produced. We do not ask you to create an account.
  • Images. If you use the video feature, the images you upload are used only to render your video.
  • Generated files. Audio, subtitle, image and video files are stored temporarily so you can download them and are deleted automatically about two hours after creation. We do not publish them, and we do not use your scripts or files to train models.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider's servers receive your IP address and basic browser details when you load a page. We also keep your IP address in memory for up to an hour to enforce fair-use limits (for example, how many episodes can be generated per hour). We do not build visitor profiles from this information.

Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), personalized ads and non-essential cookies are used only after you give consent through the consent message shown on the site.

The site itself does not currently use analytics cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy first.

Third-party services

Audio generation relies on a third-party text-to-speech service, and advertising (when enabled) relies on Google. These providers handle data under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not keep accounts or long-term records, there is usually nothing to delete beyond the temporary files described above. If you would like to ask what we hold about you, or request removal of anything, email us at amosbah506@gmail.com.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
