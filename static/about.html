About DuoVoice Studio

A free two-voice dialogue studio for podcasters, teachers and video creators.

What this site does

DuoVoice Studio turns a written conversation between two people into a finished audio episode. You write the script, choose two voices, and download an MP3 or WAV file with natural pacing between turns. You can add a gentle background music bed, generate a subtitle file, or combine the audio with your own images to export a ready-to-upload video.

Who it is for

  • Podcasters and YouTubers who want to hear a draft, test a format or produce a faceless channel without recording every line.
  • Language teachers and learners who need listening material at a controlled speed with matching subtitles.
  • Educators and explainer creators who present an idea as a conversation, which is often easier to follow than a monologue.

About the voices

The voices are general-purpose synthetic neural voices provided by a third-party text-to-speech service. They are not recordings of real people, and the site does not clone anyone's voice. We encourage you to tell your audience when audio is computer-generated.

Who is behind it

DuoVoice Studio is an independent project created and maintained by Ahmed, Founder. The guides in the blog are written to be useful on their own: practical advice on scripting, subtitles and language practice, based on how the tool actually works.
