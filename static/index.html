Tips for dialogue that sounds human

Keep turns short, let one speaker ask and the other explain, and use contractions the way people do when they talk. Read the script out loud before you generate it: if you stumble, a listener will too. Use {pause} for a beat after a surprising line, and use emotions sparingly, because a little goes a long way. Frequently asked questions

Is DuoVoice Studio really free?

Yes. There is no account and no payment. To keep the service available for everyone, each script has a maximum length and each visitor can generate a limited number of episodes per hour.

Are the voices real people?

No. They are synthetic neural voices from a third-party text-to-speech service. They are not recordings of real people, and the studio does not clone voices.

Why only two speakers?

Two-person conversations cover most interviews, explainers and language dialogues, and keeping to two speakers keeps the studio fast and simple.

Which languages are supported?

There are quick filters for English, Arabic, Japanese, French, Spanish and German, and the “All languages” option lists every voice the voice service offers.

Can I use the audio commercially?

You keep ownership of your script and you are responsible for how you use the result. Because the voices come from a third-party service, please check that service's terms before using audio in a commercial project. See our Terms of Service.

How long are my files kept?

Generated files are deleted automatically about two hours after creation, so download what you need right away.
